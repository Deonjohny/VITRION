SOURCE AUDIT · ADULT REFERENCE

Anatomy sources & coverage

The target inventory contains 206 bones, following the standard adult counting convention. Natural variation exists. This preview is an incomplete adult reference and has not received VITRION anatomical approval.

What is actually in the mesh?

198 bone mesh nodes correspond to 196 inventory bones. The manubrium, sternal body and xiphoid process are selected together as one sternum. All correspondence is a candidate for expert review. Source FMA identifiers, names and laterality are checked against the actual GLB.

Ten missing inventory bones

Left and right malleus, incus and stapes; hyoid; coccyx; left and right triquetrum. These are marked unavailable. No invented meshes are substituted.

Human geometry and its limits

The approved Écorché source derives its bone surfaces from BodyParts3D. It is a single adult male reference, simplified for real-time display. Its published processing mirrors the right side from the left and softens fine surface detail. It cannot establish natural individual asymmetry, pediatric anatomy, internal bone microstructure, or patient-specific geometry. Image-space findings do not acquire anatomical 3D coordinates from this atlas.

Landmark coordinates, physical registration transforms and clinical geometry remain unregistered. Inventory tests verify naming and counts; they do not constitute an expert review of shape, proportions, joint articulation or landmark placement.
